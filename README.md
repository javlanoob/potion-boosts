# Potion Boost
Draws the skill icons a potion in your inventory would move over the potion itself, each with how much it would give, or the level it would take you to.

- The numbers come from the same item data the Item Stats plugin reads, and they are coloured the way that plugin colours its own, in the colours you have set there: green for a boost you would get all of, yellow for one that would partly go to waste, red for what a potion takes away.
- A row disappears once there is nothing left in that potion to gain, and comes back as the boost wears off.
- Only what you brew is labelled, which is anything carrying its doses in its name, and the butterflies and moths you catch. Food and drink can be turned on as well.
- Only the combat skills are labelled until you ask for the rest, and what a potion drains is left out until you ask for that.
- Carrying several of the same potion labels the one with the least left in it, which is the one worth drinking first. When two are down to the same dose it is the one nearest the top left of your inventory, or the bottom right if you would rather. Different potions are labelled separately, so a super combat and a ranging potion both get their own.
- It is drawn under the other things that label a slot, so a dose count in the corner stays readable.
